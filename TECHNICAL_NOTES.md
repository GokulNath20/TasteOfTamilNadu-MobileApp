# Behind the App: Taste of Tamil Nadu

A short look at how the app came together and what is still in progress.

---

## One App, Two Phones

The app is built once and runs on both Android and iPhone, so guests on either platform get the same welcome, menu and story.

---

## What Guests See

* **A warm welcome:** the opening slides introduce the cafe, the coffee and tea, and the meals before a guest steps in.
* **A menu that is easy to scan:** dishes are grouped into four categories, each with photos. Sold-out dishes stay visible so guests know before they ask.
* **Clear sign-up:** guests start with a phone number, confirm a short code, and finish their profile.
* **Feedback that feels personal:** signed-in guests can share their thoughts from the About Us tab.

---

## Still in Progress

Some parts of the app are placeholders for now:

* **Menu:** the dishes and prices are sample content until the restaurant's real menu is confirmed.
* **Sign-up:** the verification code is not checked yet and no text message is sent, so any code is accepted. Accounts are not kept between app launches.
* **Birthdate:** guests type the date. A date picker is planned.
* **Feedback:** messages are not saved yet. Guests see a thank-you message after sending.
* **Appearance:** the app uses one fixed look and does not follow the phone's light or dark setting.
